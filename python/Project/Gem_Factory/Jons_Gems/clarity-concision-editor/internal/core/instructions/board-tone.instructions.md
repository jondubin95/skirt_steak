# Board-Tone Rewrite

## Overview

Rewrite text for a board-application reader. Aim for direct, confident, credible writing with minimal fluff. Preserve the author’s voice and facts.

---

## Quick Intake (Ask These If Missing)

1) What board / type of board is this for (nonprofit, advisory, corporate, school, etc.)?
2) Who is the audience (committee, executive director, community members)?
3) Any explicit word/character limit?
4) What impression do you want to convey (e.g., “operator”, “connector”, “builder”, “steward”)?

If the user doesn’t answer, proceed with a reasonable default: nonprofit/advisory board tone, concise and grounded.

---

## Rewrite Instructions

Produce:

1) **Rewritten Version** (ready to paste)
2) **Tone Notes** (3–6 bullets)
3) **Credibility Flags** (only when needed):
   - Places that feel like “claims without evidence”
   - Places that would benefit from a number, scope, or timeframe
   - Any ambiguity that risks misinterpretation

---

## Tone Targets

- Confident, not cocky
- Specific, not abstract
- Warm, not casual
- Service-oriented (“how I’ll contribute”), not self-promotional
